1. Introduction

Welcome to Ashward ("we," "our," or "us"). Ashward is a mobile application designed to help users on their journey to quit smoking. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our application.

By using Ashward, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Who We Are

Ashward is operated as an independent project. For all privacy-related inquiries, you can reach us at:

Email: ataseversoftware@gmail.com

3. Age Restriction

Ashward is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If you are under 18, please do not use Ashward.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account and use Ashward, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and (if signing in with Google) profile photo
  • Smoking Habits Data: Quit date, daily cigarette consumption, cigarettes per pack, price per pack
  • Personal Goals: Reasons for quitting, wishlist rewards (items you want to purchase with saved money), savings targets
  • Usage History: Slip-up records (when you smoke after quitting), optional notes about slip-ups

4.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Timezone: Used only on your device to schedule local notifications at the right local time — it is not sent to our servers
  • Crash Reports: When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects device model, operating system version, app version, and the cause of the crash to help us fix bugs
  • Purchase Diagnostics: RevenueCat records device and app version information as part of your purchase/subscription history to support and troubleshoot transactions
  • Authentication Tokens: Session tokens to keep you logged in

4.3 Information from Third-Party Services

If you sign in with Google, we receive: - Email address - Display name - Profile picture URL - Google Account ID

If you sign in with Apple, we receive: - Email address (or a private relay address, if you choose to hide your email) - Full name (only if you choose to share it on your first sign-in) - Apple User ID

4.4 Purchase Information

If you purchase a premium subscription: - Anonymous purchase identifier (managed by RevenueCat) - Purchase history (subscription start date, renewal status) - We do not see or store your credit card details — these are processed entirely by the Apple App Store

5. Special Category Data (Health Information)

Smoking habits and quit-smoking journey data may be considered health-related information under GDPR (Article 9) and similar regulations. We process this data based on your explicit consent when you sign up.

This data is used exclusively to provide you with personalized quit-smoking support and is never shared with third parties for marketing or research purposes without your additional explicit consent.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • ✅ Provide and maintain the Ashward service
  • ✅ Calculate personalized statistics (cigarettes avoided, money saved, life regained)
  • ✅ Send you motivational notifications at scheduled times
  • ✅ Sync your data across devices
  • ✅ Process premium subscriptions
  • ✅ Diagnose and fix technical issues (via crash reports)
  • ✅ Communicate with you about service updates or support requests
  • ✅ Display anonymous community statistics (e.g., total registered users)

We never: - ❌ Sell your personal data to third parties - ❌ Use your data for advertising purposes - ❌ Share your individual smoking history with anyone

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  1. Open Ashward
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm

Alternatively, email us at ataseversoftware@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

When you delete your account, the following happens immediately, in this order:

  1. Your feedback messages and your users data (quit date, settings, rewards, premium/trial status) are deleted from Firestore
  2. If you signed in with Apple, your Apple sign-in authorization is revoked
  3. Your authentication account is permanently deleted
  4. Locally on your device, scheduled notifications are cancelled and all cached app data (including local high scores and diagnostic logs) is cleared

If any of these steps fails (for example, due to no internet connection), the process stops and you are shown an error so you can try again — your account is not left in a partially-deleted state without your knowledge.

13. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • 🔐 All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • 🔐 Firebase security rules restrict data access to authenticated users only
  • 🔐 You can only access your own data, not other users'
  • 🔐 Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text (handled by Firebase)
  • 🔐 Regular security audits via Firebase's built-in security tools

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
